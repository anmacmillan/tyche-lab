1,000 imagined worlds (Monte Carlo)

We don't know Tyche's true sickness rate on each food. So the computer imagines 1,000 worlds that fit what we've seen, and in each one checks which food is kinder to her tummy.
 
Each dot is one imagined world, coloured by the winning food. Below, every world drops its two imagined rates into the bars: they pile up into the same shapes as the belief curves. That's how scientists turn "we saw this" into "we're this sure".
